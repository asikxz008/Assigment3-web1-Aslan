Three-day sightseeing ideas for Almaty, Astana, and Turkistan
Destination Day 1 Day 2 Day 3
Almaty Walk through Panfilov Park and explore the Green Bazaar. Discover the mountain area around Medeu and Shymbulak. See the city from Kok Tobe.
Astana Start at Bayterek Tower and the surrounding boulevard. Explore the National Museum of Kazakhstan. Visit Khan Shatyr and walk by the Ishim River.
Turkistan Visit the Khoja Ahmed Yasawi Mausoleum. Explore more of Turkistan's historic center. Consider a regional trip to Sauran or Otyrar.

These are sightseeing suggestions, not bookings or timed itineraries.
